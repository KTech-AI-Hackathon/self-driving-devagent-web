01 · Why / Current Challenges

The Development Bottleneck 开发瓶颈

No Autonomy 缺乏自主性

AI is a passive tool, not an active teammate. A simple login page still needs a human to prompt, review, and push at every step. AI 是被动工具而非主动队友。一个简单的登录页面，仍需人工在每一步提示、审查和推进。

Process Fragmentation 流程断裂

Requirements in Jira, design in Figma, code in IDE, tests in CI — 5 tools, 5 people, 3-5 days for one feature. 需求在 Jira、设计在 Figma、代码在 IDE、测试在 CI——5 个工具、5 个人、3-5 天完成一个功能。

Inefficient Requirements 需求低效

BA writes specs, dev reads specs, back-and-forth clarification — 80% of delay happens before a single line of code is written. BA 写需求、开发读需求、反复澄清——80% 的延迟发生在第一行代码写之前。

Limited Parallelism 并行能力不足

One developer, one task at a time. When the backlog has 50 tickets, the math doesn't add up. 一个开发者一次做一个任务。当积压 50 个工单时，传统模式根本无法应对。

🤖 vs AI Copilots: Cursor & Copilot assist — DevAgent autonomously delivers. From "AI helps you code" to "AI codes for you end-to-end" 🤖 vs AI 辅助工具：Cursor、Copilot 是辅助——DevAgent 自主交付。从"AI帮你写代码"到"AI替你端到端交付"
